Local Caption — send to chat (browser extension)

What it does
  Types the question Local Caption heard into the chat tab you already have open — ChatGPT,
  DeepSeek, Claude, Gemini or Copilot — without taking the window focus away from your call.
  It talks to nothing except Local Caption on this PC (http://127.0.0.1).

Install (Chrome, Edge, Brave — once)
  1. Open chrome://extensions   (Edge: edge://extensions)
  2. Turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
  3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose this folder.
  4. In Local Caption: Settings > Send > "Browser extension, or your own tool" > Save.
  5. Reload the chat tab once, so the extension's script is in it.

Use
  Press your "Send last question" key in Local Caption (F9 unless you changed it).
  Tick "Press Enter after the text goes in" in Settings > Send to have it submitted too.

If nothing arrives
  - Is the chat tab open, and reloaded since installing?
  - Does the port in the extension's options match Settings > Send (17653 by default)?
  - Chat sites change their pages. If the text stops landing in the box, switch Local Caption to
    "Paste into a window" — it does not depend on the page's markup at all.
